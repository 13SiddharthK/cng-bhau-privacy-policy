## 1. Introduction

CNG भाऊ ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free mobile application that helps users find nearby CNG fuel stations in India. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using CNG भाऊ, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### Location Data
We request access to your device's location (GPS) **only while you are using the app**. This is used solely to:
- Show CNG stations near your current location
- Calculate approximate distances to each station
- Display your position on the map

We do **not** store, share, or track your location in the background. Location data is used in real-time and is never saved to our servers.

### Device Information
We may collect basic, anonymous device information (such as device type and OS version) through third-party analytics tools to improve app performance.

### Camera
The app requests camera permission to support QR code scanning features. Camera data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

---

## 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information without your explicit consent during account registration.
- We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to any third party.
- We do not track your location in the background.
